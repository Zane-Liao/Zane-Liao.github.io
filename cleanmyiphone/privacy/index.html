Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

CleanMyIPhone is designed with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and protected when you use the App.

✓

Your files stay on your device.
Core photo, video, and file processing happens locally.

1. Privacy at a Glance

  • Photos, videos, files, and media conversions are processed locally on your device.
  • Your personal files are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use your files for advertising.
  • You remain in control of permissions granted to the App.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account. The developer does not operate a server that collects or stores the contents of your photos, videos, documents, audio files, or other personal files. Information on your device is accessed only when necessary to provide features you choose to use.

3. Photos and Videos

If you grant photo-library access, the App may access photos and videos to identify large media, display and organize media, help you review items for deletion, analyze file characteristics, and convert supported media formats. This processing occurs locally on your device, and the contents of your photo library are not uploaded to developer-operated servers.

You may change or revoke photo access at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Files and Folders

The App may let you select files or folders through Apple’s system file picker. It accesses only items made available through your choices and permissions. Browsing, organizing, deleting, and converting supported files occurs on your device.

The App may keep local references needed to remember items you previously authorized. These references remain on your device.

5. Media Conversion

Supported image, video, audio, and other media conversions are performed locally on your device unless the App explicitly states otherwise. Files submitted for conversion are not transmitted to developer-operated servers.

6. File Deletion

Deletion occurs only after an action you initiate or confirm. Depending on the content type and Apple’s system behavior, deleted photos or videos may remain temporarily in the system’s “Recently Deleted” area. You are responsible for reviewing items before confirming deletion.

7. Purchases and Subscriptions

Paid features, in-app purchases, or subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card or banking information. The App may receive information from StoreKit needed to determine purchase or subscription status. Apple handles such information under its own privacy policies and terms.

8. Analytics and Advertising

The App does not use personal files for advertising or behavioral profiling, sell personal information, or use third-party advertising SDKs to track users across apps or websites. If analytics or diagnostic services are introduced, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as required.

9. Device Permissions

The App may request access to Photos, files and folders, or other system resources needed for a feature you explicitly request. Permissions are controlled by Apple’s operating system and may be changed or revoked in Settings. Features that depend on a revoked permission may stop working.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because core processing occurs locally, the developer generally retains no external copies of your personal or converted files. Preferences, settings, cached information, and authorized-file references may remain on your device until removed by the App, reset by you, or deleted when the App is uninstalled, subject to iOS behavior.

You may remove local App data using available App controls or by uninstalling the App, and may revoke system permissions in iOS Settings.

11. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple technologies and system services that may include the App Store, StoreKit, Photos, Files, and document-picker services. Information processed directly by Apple is governed by Apple’s privacy practices. If other services that process user information are added, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

12. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through developer-operated servers.

13. International Users

Privacy and consumer-protection rights vary by location. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

14. Security

Keeping file processing on-device reduces the personal content transmitted over the internet, but no software or device can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, Apple Account, and backups.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App, its features, or legal requirements change. The “Last Updated” date will be revised, and material changes may also be communicated through the App when appropriate.
